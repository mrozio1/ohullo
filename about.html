The story so far

I craft operational experiences that merge functionality with employee satisfaction.

My journey began over three decades ago, when I discovered a deep desire to know how things work. Since then I've worked with startups and Fortune 500 companies alike, always bringing a detail-oriented, people-first approach to the table.

I believe good design is invisible: it just works. My approach is rooted in minimalism, stripping away the non-essential to reveal the core purpose of a product. Whether it's a multilayered global deployment or a simple dashboard of call metrics, I aim for clarity, speed and ease of use.

Today I'm Director of Digital Strategy at MCDP. I set the group's digital direction, give advanced technical guidance to the people running each practice, and steer how its technology grows, after first leading its marketing as Director of Marketing.
