One partner from the first conversation to the result you can measure. Here's how every engagement runs.

  1. ohullo

    You bring the idea or the problem. You get one point of contact who owns the whole path.

    No hand-offs between agencies, no chasing five vendors. Ohullo coordinates every specialist and tool, and answers to you.

  2. Requirement gathering

    We map what you need, who it's for and what success looks like, before anyone writes code or signs a contract.

    • Stakeholder interviews and a current-state walkthrough
    • Goals written as measurable outcomes
    • A scope you can approve in plain language

  3. Tech stack review

    An honest look at the systems you already run: what to keep, what to connect and what to retire.

    • An inventory of tools, data and integrations
    • Security and risk notes
    • Quick wins you can act on right away

  4. Vendor selection

    The right tools from a trusted network of AI and technology providers, chosen for fit, not for hype.

    • A short list with side-by-side trade-offs
    • Real cost, including licenses and upkeep
    • A recommendation you can defend to leadership

  5. Implementation

    Build, integrate and launch, with your team trained along the way so nothing depends on one person.

    • Phased rollout with checkpoints
    • Integration with the systems you keep
    • Training and written runbooks

  6. Transparent project reporting

    Plain-language updates on progress, budget and risks, so you always know where things stand.

    • Regular status updates you can forward as-is
    • Milestones tracked against the original scope
    • Risks raised early, with options

  7. Achieved outcome

    A measured result you can point to, and a team that knows how to run what was built.

    For example: a front-end AI agent that took a call center's speed of answer from 57 seconds to 0, with QA running on every ticket instead of a 5% sample.
