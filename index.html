    I'm Mark Johnson, welcome.

    • Operational mastery 20+ years
    • Customer advocate always
    • Your best decision see chapter five

    This is a short book about one long habit: figuring out how things work, then making them work better.

    That habit started more than three decades ago, and it's taken me through startups and Fortune 500 companies alike. I've opened deployment centers on four continents, run the largest one-call center in the country, and, most recently, turned the same discipline toward marketing and the web.

    I believe good design is invisible. It just works. Whether it's a multilayered global deployment or a simple dashboard that shows call metrics, I strip away what isn't essential until the core purpose is obvious to everyone who touches it.

    The pages ahead cover the numbers, the jobs, a letter I wrote, and, in chapter five, a page that writes itself around whatever problem you came here with.

    Chapter four

    A letter

    Tucked between the pages. It says what I believe better than a résumé can.

    Fellow American,

    I'm not a politician. I'll never run for office. I don't have a law degree or a family name that opens doors. What I have is over a decade of experience fixing broken systems, leading teams through crises, and delivering results when the stakes are real.

    I scaled operations 700%. I cut costs by $1.6M while improving quality. I managed teams across 8 countries. I kept operations running through a pandemic without shutting down once. I implemented AI that works, not AI that sounds good in a pitch deck.

    I learned that process beats politics. That data trumps promises. That accountability matters more than excuses. And that leadership means showing up when things are hard, not just when cameras are rolling.

    Companies don't need more speeches. They need better systems. Transparent metrics. Real accountability. Teams built for competence, not connections. Technology from this decade, not the 1970s. I'm not promising perfection. I'm promising discipline, transparency, and an employee that actually works.

    I'm Mark Johnson. Father of three girls. Devoted husband. Operations executive. Texan and Red Blooded American. I'm tired of watching capable people get buried by broken systems while others argue about who's to blame. Let's fix it together.

    Mark Johnson 2026
